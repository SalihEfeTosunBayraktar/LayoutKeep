LayoutKeep keeps the layout.
The words change, the page does not.

A local-first translator for PDF, EPUB and DOCX that works on the document's own layout model: every text box keeps its position and its size, figures stay where they were, tables keep their rows, and what does not fit is flagged for review instead of being silently crushed.

Every bug, written down

The project's full record in eight chapters — how it started, what was tried and abandoned, which root cause was measured for each bug, and what is still known to be broken: from zero to now.

Compare, don't take our word

Every measured document is published with its source and its translation side by side under a draggable divider — plus the audit numbers the checker produced for it.

Runs on your machine

Point it at LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. No document leaves the computer unless the endpoint you chose is remote.

Losses are reported

Ten loss criteria and three fit criteria are checked after writing: a dropped number, a moved block, text over a figure — each one lands in a review queue, not in silence.

Open sources, published pages

The comparison set is public-domain or openly licensed (NIST, NASA, PLOS, Wikipedia, Project Gutenberg). Copyrighted test documents stay on the author's disk.

LayoutKeep is in active development. The comparison site shows the current engine's output on documents it was not tuned for.
